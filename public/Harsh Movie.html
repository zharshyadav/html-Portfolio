Best Movies according to Harsh

My Top 5 Movies of all times

Avengers: Endgame

It has great action sequences and a satisfying conclusion to the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

The Batman

A dark and gritty take on the iconic superhero, with excellent performances and cinematography.

Inception

A mind-bending thriller that explores the concept of dreams and reality in a unique way.

Interstellar

A epic science fiction film that follows a group of explorers who travel through a wormhole in space in search of humanity's new home.

Joker

A psychological thriller that delves into the origin story of the iconic DC villain, with a powerful performance by Joaquin Phoenix.
